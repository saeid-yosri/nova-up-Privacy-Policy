Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 10, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Nova UP / Shark Gym mobile application (collectively, the "Application") operated by saeid yosri ("Service Provider").

1. Critical Device Permissions

To provide core gym check-in services, the Application requests the following sensitive features only while you are actively using the app (Foreground):

A. Precise Location Access (GPS):

The Application accesses your device's precise location (via ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION) only at the exact moment you scan the QR code. This is strictly used to verify that you are physically present inside the designated gym branch, preventing fraudulent remote check-ins. We do NOT track or store your location in the background.

B. Camera Access:

The Application requires access to your device's camera (via CAMERA) solely to scan the physical QR Code printed at the gym reception for attendance logging. No personal photos or media files are ever captured, read, or transmitted.

2. Information Collection and Use

When you download and use the Application, it may automatically process basic system metadata to ensure service stability, including:

  • Your device's Internet Protocol (IP) address.
  • The mobile operating system type and system logs.
  • App interaction timestamps (e.g., successful check-in logs).

For an optimal experience, you or your Gym administration may provide certain personally identifiable information, including but not limited to your Name, Email (saeid.yosri@gmail.com), and Phone Number (+201553650426) to verify your gym subscription.

3. Third-Party Services

The Application utilizes trusted third-party SDKs provided by Google to handle infrastructure, analytics, and performance. These services process data independently according to their own privacy frameworks:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google Analytics for Firebase
  • Firebase Crashlytics

4. Data Retention Policy

The Service Provider retains your data based on operational necessity:

  • User Provided Data: Retained for the duration of your active gym subscription plus 12 months thereafter, unless immediate deletion is requested.
  • Automatically Collected Data: Diagnostic logs are retained for up to 24 months for system optimization.

5. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You maintain full ownership of your data. You may request access to, correction of, or permanent deletion of your personal records at any time. To exercise these rights, please contact the Service Provider directly at saeid.yosri@gmail.com.

6. Security Measures

We implement professional electronic, procedural, and physical safeguards to defend your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration.
